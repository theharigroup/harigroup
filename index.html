About Us.

Led by Principal Investigator Durga Prasad Hari, The Hari Group is a dynamic and collaborative team of research associates alongside graduate, master’s, and undergraduate students. The group's research focuses on pioneering mild, sustainable reaction paradigms that provide strategic access to versatile reactive intermediates, specifically leveraging the unique reactivity of strained systems and carbene intermediates activated by light and electricity to construct valuable molecular architectures.

About the Hari Group

The Hari Group, led by Prof. Durga Prasad Hari, is a synthetic organic chemistry research group in the Department of Organic Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, India. We develop new reactions that harness the energy of ring strain, light and electricity, using strained rings, diazo compounds, metal carbenes and electrosynthesis to build high-value molecules.
