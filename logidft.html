Your Data Stays with You

Files are parsed locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Your Gaussian/ORCA log files and all results are processed entirely on your own computer. LogiDFT has no upload, no server-side storage and no login, so your unpublished data never leaves your device. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything; only display preferences (theme, units) are remembered in your browser.
