Research Areas & Methodologies

Pillar 01

Ring-Strain Release Chemistry

Strained organic architectures store significant thermodynamic strain energy. By initiating selective C-C bond cleavage in systems like [1.1.1]propellane, we unlock novel ring-expansion and cross-coupling pathways to produce valuable bioisosteres (e.g., bicyclo[1.1.1]pentanes).

Key Features
• Interrupted Dowd-Beckwith
• 65 kcal/mol Strain Release
• Saturated BCP Bioisosteres
Pillar 02

Diazo & Carbene Reagents

We design stable, bench-friendly carbene precursor reagents. These enable late-stage C-H insertions, sigmatropic rearrangements, and functionalizations without requiring hazardous transient diazo handling.

Key Features
• [2,3]-Sigmatropic Rearrangement
• Sulfoxonium Ylide Activation
• Nitrogen Extrusion
Pillar 03

Visible-Light Photoredox Catalysis

Harnessing visible LEDs (400-450 nm) alongside transition-metal or organic photocatalysts to generate reactive radical intermediates under ambient temperature conditions.

Key Features
• Blue LED (450 nm) Reactivity
• Single Electron Transfer (SET)
• Dual Catalytic Systems
Pillar 04

Electro-Organic Synthesis

Replacing chemical oxidizing/reducing agents with clean electricity. Using constant current electrolysis, we perform reagent-free ring expansions and bond formations.

Key Features
• ElectraSyn 2.0 Workstations
• Constant Current Electrolysis
• Green Reagent-Free Reactions
