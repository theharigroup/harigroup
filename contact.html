Graduate Students

Students interested in pursuing their graduate studies with us should apply to the Ph.D. program in chemistry at IISc Bangalore.

Prospective graduate students are encouraged to email D. P. Hari.

Postdoc
02

Postdoctoral Associates

Applications should be sent via email to D. P. Hari directly. Please include in the application package a curriculum vitae, 2-3 representative publications and the contact information of 2 professional references.

BS & MS
03

BS & MS Students

Undergraduate students interested in research in organic chemistry, catalysis, and electrochemistry are welcome, and please send a curriculum vitae and current transcript to D. P. Hari.
